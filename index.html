Veyro & Digital Saver

A privacy-first smartwatch and its companion app

Veyro is a health-tracking watch built on ESP32 hardware, and Digital Saver is the Flutter app that pairs with it over Bluetooth. History lives on your phone, there is no account, and everything works without a cloud service.

  • Live and stored heart rate, SpO2 estimate, HRV, steps and activity
  • Health hub: hydration, caffeine, fasting timer, mood, workouts and breathing guides
  • Alarms, reminders and medication times, also synced to the watch
  • App lock, emergency contact card and watch-face preview

Max Tools

All-in-one toolkit for developers, freelancers and business users

One searchable, keyboard-driven app that replaces a pile of single-purpose utilities. Press Ctrl+K, type what you want in plain English, and it picks the right tool, even when you mistype.

  • Variables carry one tool's result into the next

Our story

Built by a student who got tired of renting software

Cambric was founded by Asser Karim while still a student. The idea is simple: the apps you use should run on your own hardware, keep your data with you, and keep working when the internet or the company does not.

So we build real products instead of demos: a smartwatch ecosystem, an offline toolkit, a creative suite, and a web-discovery robot. The long-term goal is Talven, a local AI platform you buy once and truly own.

Revenue is meant to fund better hardware, research and new products, not luxury.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is Cambric software free?

Web Explorer is free to use. Max Tools and the other apps may be installed and used free of charge on your own devices for personal, educational or internal business use. Talven's Ember models are planned to be free forever, with Forge, Atlas and Crown as one-time purchases.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Not by Max Tools, C-Editor or Web Explorer. Your data stays on your device or in your browser. One exception: Veyro's assistant screen calls a hosted endpoint, and the app tells you when it does.

Is the code open source?

No. The products are proprietary, with source published for inspection and personal use under each repository's license. We would rather say so plainly.

Which platforms are supported?

Max Tools targets Windows, Linux and Android. C-Editor is for Windows. Digital Saver is a Flutter app. Web Explorer runs in any modern browser.

Is the Veyro watch available to buy?

Not yet. Veyro is a prototype and a design goal. The companion app works today, and the watch firmware has not been tested on production hardware.

When will Talven launch?

Talven is in the design stage, so there is no release date. It will start as a desktop app that runs models on your own computer.

How can I report a security issue?

Please do not open a public issue. Each repository has a SECURITY.md that explains how to report one privately.
